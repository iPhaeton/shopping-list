## What we collect and why

**Your email address.** We use it to create your account, send you sign-in codes, and tell you
about important changes to the service.
Legal basis: performing our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**Your display name.** You choose it after your first sign-in. Other users use it to find you
and share lists with you, and it is shown to the people on your lists.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

**Information from Google or Apple, if you sign in with them.** Google sends us your email
address, your name and a link to your profile picture. Apple sends your email address (or a
private relay address, if you choose to hide yours) and, the first time only, your name. Our
sign-in system stores what they send with your account. The app uses only the name, and only to
suggest a display name.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

**Your lists.** This means list names, items, whether an item is done, when lists and items were
created, completed or deleted, who is on each list, and what role each person has.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

**An Apple sign-in token, if you sign in with Apple.** We keep the token Apple issues so that,
when you delete your account, we can tell Apple to remove ShoppingLoop's access to your Apple ID.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

**Sign-in sessions and technical logs.** This means your IP address, the type of device or
browser, and the times you sign in and use the service. We use them to keep your account secure,
to let you sign out of all your devices, and to find and fix faults.
Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working
(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Your subscription status.** This means which plan you have, when it renews or ends, and a
random customer ID linked to your account. It comes from Apple through RevenueCat. Apple handles
the payment, so we never see your card or bank details.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

**Messages you send to support.** This means your email address and what you write.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when your message is about your account or the service, and
otherwise our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You need an email address (or a Google or Apple account) to have an account, and a display name
to use the app. Without them, we can't provide the service. Everything else you add is up to you.

**What we don't do.** We don't show ads, track you across other apps or websites, or sell your
data, and the app contains no analytics or advertising tools. The app doesn't ask for your
location, contacts, photos or camera. The Auto theme estimates daylight from your device's time
zone, and it does this on the device.

## Who can see your data

- **Every ShoppingLoop user** can find you by your display name when sharing a list, and sees
  your display name only.
- **People on a list with you** see the list's contents and everyone's display name, email
  address and role.
- **Service providers** that process data for us under data processing agreements:
  - **Supabase**: the database, sign-in and live sync. Your account and lists are stored in
    Ireland (EU).
  - **Resend**: sends sign-in code emails and our replies to support email.
  - **Cloudflare**: hosts this website and forwards email sent to support@shopping-loop.com.
  - **Google (Gmail)**: the mailbox where support email is kept.
  - **RevenueCat**: keeps track of subscription status.
- **Independent controllers**, which use your data under their own privacy policies:
  - **Apple**: the App Store, payments and Sign in with Apple. Apple sells the subscription to you.
  - **Google**: Sign in with Google, if you use it.
- **Public authorities**, when the law requires us to share data.

## Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Resend, Cloudflare, Google and RevenueCat are US companies. Supabase is too, but it stores your
account and lists in the EU. When data reaches the US or another country outside the EEA, it is
protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified, or by the
European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Write to us for a copy of the safeguards.

## How long we keep data

- **Your account, display name and lists:** for as long as you have an account.
- **Deleted lists and items:** they go to the bin ("Show deleted") and are erased permanently
  30 days after you delete them.
- **When you delete your account:** your account, email address, display name and sessions are
  deleted at once. Every list you are the only owner of is also deleted, including shared ones,
  so their other members lose them too. Lists with another owner stay with that owner. Items you
  added to other people's lists stay in those lists, because they belong to the list, not to you.
  If you signed in with Apple, we revoke the Apple token first, then delete it.
- **Technical logs and backups:** up to 7 days.
- **Support email:** up to 2 years after the last message. We keep it longer only if we need it
  to establish or defend a legal claim.

## Data stored on your device

To work without an internet connection, the app stores the following on your device: your
sign-in session, a copy of your lists, changes not yet sent, your display name, and your sort
and theme settings.

- **Signing out** removes the copy of your lists and your display name. Changes not yet sent stay
  until you sign in to the same account again, so they aren't lost.
- **Deleting your account** removes all of it.
- **Deleting the app** removes all of it too.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** your data and get a copy of it;
- **correct** it. You can change your display name in the app (Account → Edit name). For anything
  else, write to us;
- **erase** it. In the app, go to Account → Delete account, or write to us;
- **restrict** how we use it;
- **object** to uses based on our legitimate interest;
- **data portability**: write to us and we will send your lists in a common, machine-readable
  format;
- **complain to a supervisory authority**: in Poland, the President of the Personal Data
  Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa,
  [uodo.gov.pl](https://uodo.gov.pl)), or the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

To use these rights, write to support@shopping-loop.com, preferably from the email address on
your account so we can confirm it's you. We answer within one month.

We make no decisions about you by automated means alone, and we don't profile you.

## Children

ShoppingLoop is not meant for children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has given us
their data, tell us and we will delete it.

## Security

Every connection between the app and our servers is encrypted. Access rules in the database let
only a list's members read it. Sign-in codes can be used only once and expire. You can sign out of
all your devices at any time (Account → Sign out of all devices). No system is completely secure,
so if you find a problem, please tell us at support@shopping-loop.com.

## This website

shopping-loop.com uses no cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking. Cloudflare, which
serves the site, processes your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks.
Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a
change matters to how your data is used, we tell you in the app or by email before it takes
effect.
