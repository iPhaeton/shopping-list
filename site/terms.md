## 2. What the service does

ShoppingLoop keeps shopping lists in sync between your devices and the people you share them
with. With an account, you can:

- create lists, and add, check off, rename and delete items;
- restore deleted lists and items from the bin for 30 days, after which they are erased;
- share a list with other ShoppingLoop users by their display name, as a Reader (can view), a
  Writer (can also change items) or an Owner (can also rename and delete the list and decide who
  is on it);
- keep working without an internet connection. Your changes are saved on the device and sent
  when the connection returns.

**Limits.** You can own up to 100 lists, be on up to 1,000 lists in total, and keep up to 1,000
items in a list. Lists and items in the bin don't count toward these limits.

**Price.** The service is free, except for the optional subscription described in section 6.
