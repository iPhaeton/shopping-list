## Common questions

**I didn't get my sign-in code.**
Check your spam folder. The code comes from no-reply@mail.shopping-loop.com. Codes expire, so if
an old one doesn't work, ask for a new one. Never give your code to anyone. We will never ask for
it.

**How do I share a list?**
Open the list, tap **Share list**, and search for the person by their display name. Choose what
they can do:

- **Reader** can view the list.
- **Writer** can also add, check off and change items.
- **Owner** can also rename and delete the list and decide who is on it.

**Someone added me to a list I don't want.**
Open the list, tap **Share list**, then **Leave list**. If someone keeps adding you, write to us.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Turn on **Show deleted** to see the bin, then restore what you need. Deleted lists and items stay
there for 30 days, then they are erased for good. Only a list's owners can restore a list.

**How do I change my display name?**
Tap **Account**, then **Edit name**.

**How do I delete my account?**
Tap **Account**, then **Delete account**. This is immediate and permanent. Every list you are the
only owner of is deleted, for everyone on it. To keep a shared list alive, make someone else an
owner first. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open iPhone **Settings** → your name → **Subscriptions**, and choose ShoppingLoop. Apple handles
all payments. To ask for a refund, go to
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**I changed phones and my subscription is missing.**
Sign in with the same ShoppingLoop account, then tap **Restore purchases** on the Account screen.
